How much will my roof cost? +

We give a written line-item estimate after the free 21-point inspection. Price depends on gauge (0.40 / 0.50 / 0.60), foam, truss span and gutter type. No hidden charges — you approve before we start.

What does the 1 year warranty on labor cover? +

Labor warranty covers leaks and workmanship defects. If there's a leak due to installation within 1 year, we come back and fix it. Material warranty follows the manufacturer's terms.

How long does installation take? +

Most single-story homes: 3–7 days for full replacement after materials arrive. We give a schedule in your written estimate and update you daily.

Do you do free site visits? +

Yes — free 21-point inspection and measurement in Cavite and Metro Manila, including Imus, Bacoor, Dasma, General Trias, Tanza, Kawit, Biñan, Carmona, QC, Caloocan, Makati and Parañaque. Written estimate within 24 hours.

What happens after I request an estimate? +

1) We confirm by call/text within hours. 2) Schedule free inspection. 3) Send written estimate in 24H. 4) You approve — we schedule build.

How do I know you use the right materials? +

We show the actual gauge and stainless 304 stamp on site, plus photos during build (trusses, overlap, flashing). Spec is listed in your estimate — e.g., 0.50mm / 0.60mm ribtype with 10mm foam.
