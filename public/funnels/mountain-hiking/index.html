Common questions,
honest answers.

Beginner ka? Solo joiner? First time sa Benguet? Coordinator assists from pick-up to summit. If you don't see your question, message us.

Kaya ba ng beginner or walang exercise?

Yes. Difficulty 3 out of 9, four hours at chill pace with guides. Many first-timers and barkada joiners do this trail every weekend.

What should I bring?

1.5L water, trail snacks, headlamp or flashlight, cap, rain jacket, hiking shoes or sturdy sneakers, extra shirt in a ziplock, small towel, powerbank, pocket money for meals and wash-up, personal meds, and a valid ID.

What if it rains or I need to cancel?

Light rain won't stop the trip. Your guide will reroute as needed. Heavy rain or a no-go signal from the LGU: we reschedule your slot free of charge. Personal cancellations 7 or more days out get a full DP refund.

GC How do I pay the half DP?

Send P1,000 to GCash, screenshot the receipt, then send it via Messenger. The GCash number is shown on the thank-you page right after you choose a date.

Solo joiner allowed? What about meals?

Yes. Roughly half our joiners go solo. We match solo joiners with same-gender roommates for the van. Meals aren't included, so bring pocket money. Coordinators will point you to clean, cheap stops along the trail.

Is it legit and safe?

We've run weekly Ulap tours for 3+ years. DOT-accredited, registered with the LGU, environmental fees handled, guides are first-aid trained, and the vans are insured with seatbelts.
