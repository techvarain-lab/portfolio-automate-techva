Why the plan always breaks

Nothing here is about effort. It is about the three reasons a plan stops working in week three.

01

Every template assumes a life you do not have

A plan written for someone with four spare hours a week does not survive a real calendar. This one is built around the hours you actually have, then adjusted when those hours change.

02

Nobody adjusts it after week one

Something always changes. Work gets heavy, sleep breaks down, a shoulder hurts, a trip eats your training week. Calls two and three exist to fix exactly that, not to congratulate you.

03

You restart every Monday and call it discipline

Restarting is expensive. It costs motivation, it costs the progress you already made, and it costs your belief that this time is different. A plan you can follow beats a perfect plan you abandon.
